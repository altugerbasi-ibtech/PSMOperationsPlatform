  Collector execution identities; approved separately produced SQL identity
  evidence is required and missing evidence blocks readiness.
- Portal TCP validation is not inferred because the shared configuration has
  no approved Portal port; Portal configuration remains `NOT_APPLICABLE` for
  that edge.

  cannot yield a ready decision.
- The decision is a preflight assessment, not permission to execute or proof
  of a completed inventory workflow.
