# WP-005.6 - Volume Inventory

`VolumeInventoryModule` uses an explicit `MSFT_Volume` projection for UniqueId,
DriveLetter, FileSystem, FileSystemLabel, Size and SizeRemaining.

`UniqueId` is the target-scoped `StableSourceKey`. Drive Letter is mutable and
never identity. References:
[MSFT_StorageObject](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/storage/msft-storageobject)
and [MSFT_Volume](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/storage/msft-volume).

Drive-letter changes do not change identity. Volume recreation or
storage-provider remapping can change `UniqueId`; WP-005 does not claim
immutability across those lifecycle events.

DriveLetter is normalized only from an uppercase CIM Char16; null/zero means no
drive letter. FileSystemLabel maps to Label. Capacity values are non-negative
and free space cannot exceed size. Successful empty clears prior volumes;
invalid, failed or cancelled collection does not persist.

Disk and Volume remain separate snapshot units because the implemented model
has no foreign key or normalized relationship between them.
